# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small sales teams, two to twenty people, at agencies, SaaS start-ups and service businesses.

- **Sales reps** live in the pipeline and the task list. They qualify leads, move deals and log the next call, usually between meetings.
- **Sales managers** read the dashboard each morning, check the forecast, see who is ahead and chase what is overdue.
- **Owners** want one number they trust: what is likely to close this month.

## Product Purpose

NexCRM Lite is the small-team edition of NexCRM: leads, a drag-and-drop pipeline, contacts, companies, tasks and a dashboard. Success means a lead never goes cold, a deal's stage is always current, and the forecast on the dashboard matches what actually closes.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, quietly confident. The voice is plain and specific, like a good sales lead: it says what happened and what to do next.

## Anti-references

- Enterprise CRM screens with forty fields per record and five levels of menus.
- Generic AI SaaS dashboards: purple-to-blue gradients, glass cards everywhere, metrics with no way to drill in.
- Toy kanban apps with emoji, confetti and bouncy motion.

## Design Principles

1. **Every number is traceable.** KPI tiles, chart segments and totals open the records behind them.
2. **The next action is one click away.** Convert, move, tick off.
3. **Dense but calm.** More rows, fewer borders, clear hierarchy.
4. **Same vocabulary everywhere.** One table, one drawer, one toast style across all modules.
5. **Destructive means deliberate.** Deletes confirm and are soft, and only managers can do them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text and controls; the lavender swatches are never used behind white text.
- Full keyboard use: Ctrl K for search, Esc closes drawers and menus, arrow keys in pickers, visible focus rings.
- Status is never colour-only: badges carry text, overdue items say "Overdue".
- `prefers-reduced-motion` turns transitions into instant changes.
